How Your Targets Are Calculated

1. Daily Calorie Intake

Maintenance Baseline: Current Weight (kg) × 33 kcal. This calculates daily energy for an active lifter training 4–5 days per week.

Your Target: We subtract your daily deficit (default: 450 kcal) to encourage steady fat loss while preserving workout performance.

2. Protein Target (2.0g / kg)

In a caloric deficit, amino acids can be oxidized for energy.

Consuming 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight optimizes Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) and halts muscle catabolism during a cut.

3. Fat Loss Velocity & Timeline

The 7,700 kcal Rule: One kilogram of body fat contains approximately 7,700 kcal of stored energy.

A 450 kcal daily deficit accumulates to 3,150 kcal weekly, producing an optimal fat reduction rate of ~0.41 kg per week.
